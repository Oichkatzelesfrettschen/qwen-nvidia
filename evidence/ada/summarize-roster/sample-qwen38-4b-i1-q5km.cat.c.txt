## Summary

**What it does overall:**

This is the source code for the Unix `cat` command, which concatenates (joins together) the contents of one or more files and prints them to the standard output. It's a simple utility that reads files and streams their contents to the screen, one after another.

---

**Key exported functions:**

| Function | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| `main(argc, argv)` | The entry point. Parses command-line options, opens files, and orchestrates the concatenation process. It handles flags like `-u` (unbuffered), `-n` (line numbers), `-b` (ignore blank lines), `-v` (show non-printable characters), `-s` (suppress blank lines), `-e` (show end-of-line markers), and `-t` (show tabs as spaces). |
