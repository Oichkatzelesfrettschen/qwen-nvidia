This is a command-line utility program called **`cat`**. Its main job is to take one or more files (or a pipe of data) and print their contents to the screen (stdout).

It acts like a "file concatenator." You can give it a list of files to read, and it outputs them one after another. It also supports various command-line flags (like `-n`, `-v`, `-s`) that allow you to control how the output looks, such as adding line numbers, handling special characters, or buffering the data.
